Privacy Policy

Text & Picture Directions · Effective October 22, 2026

Text & Picture Directions (“the app”) helps you get directions from typed text, from words in a picture, and from indoor routes you record. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking, and it does not sell or share your information with data brokers.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your location while you are using it to show where you are on the map, to find places near you, and to plan routes. Your location is not stored by the developer.

  • Apple Maps. Searches, place lookups, and walking/driving directions are handled by Apple’s MapKit service, under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Transitous (train and bus times). When you ask for train or bus directions, the app sends the starting point (the starting station, or your location rounded to about 100 meters / 330 feet), the location of the place you are going, and the time to Transitous, a free public-transit timetable service. No name, device identifier, or account is sent.

Camera and Photos

When you take or choose a picture, the words in it are read on your device using Apple’s on-device text recognition. Pictures are not uploaded. If a chosen photo contains location information (where it was taken), that location is read on your device to search near that spot. You can choose a single photo with the system photo picker; the app does not get access to your whole photo library.

Motion and Indoor Routes

When you record an indoor route, the app uses your device’s motion sensors (steps/distance, compass, and altitude for floor changes) to build the steps. Recorded indoor routes, including any photos you add to them, are saved on your device only, unless you choose to share them to the Repository.

Repository (Sharing Routes)

Saving a route to the repository saves the trouble of finding a needle in a haystack. Anyone going to that same location can get those directions for future use. The Repository is optional. Nothing is shared unless you tap to share or save a route there. When you do, the following is stored in Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit) public database and can be seen by other people using the app:

  • The route’s title, destination name, address, and location; travel mode, distance, and time; and the steps (you can choose to leave the steps out for privacy).
  • For indoor routes: the steps, any photos you attached, and the route’s location.
  • Any note you write and the name you choose to show as the author.
  • An anonymous iCloud identifier assigned by Apple to this app, used so you can see and delete your own items. It does not reveal your Apple Account name or email address.

You can delete anything you shared at any time from the Repository’s “Mine” list. If you report an item, the report (the item, its title, and the reason you chose) is stored so the developer can review it. Hiding an author only affects your device.

Information Stored on Your Device

Saved settings, recorded indoor routes, and the list of authors you hid are kept on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Children

Anyone can use the app. Children under 13 should get a parent's permission before using it. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

  • Turn location, camera, or motion access on or off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security.
  • Use the app without the Repository; it is never required.
  • Delete your shared items in the app, or contact the developer to ask for removal.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
